# Support FAQ Resolutions

This file is the source content for `resolution_articles` and `resolution_embeddings`.
Each entry has a stable resolution ID so the local embedding importer can be safely rerun.

## Tracking has not updated

Resolution-ID: 91000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000001
Category: delivery

Question: My shipment tracking has not updated. What should I do?

Answer: Carrier scans can take up to 24 hours to appear. Check the latest tracking event and allow one full day for another scan. If tracking remains unchanged after 24 hours, contact support with your order number so the shipment can be investigated.

---

## Package is late

Resolution-ID: 91000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000002
Category: delivery

Question: My package is past its estimated delivery date. When will it arrive?

Answer: Review the most recent carrier event before estimating next steps. Delivery dates are estimates and should not be guaranteed. If there has been no movement for 24 hours after the estimated date, contact support with the order number for investigation.

---

## Package marked delivered but missing

Resolution-ID: 91000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000003
Category: delivery

Question: Tracking says delivered, but I cannot find my package.

Answer: Check all entrances, secure delivery locations, household members, neighbors, and any carrier delivery photo. Allow until the end of the delivery day because some scans occur early. If the package is still missing, contact support with the order number and delivery details.

---

## Start a return

Resolution-ID: 91000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000004
Category: returns

Question: How do I return an item?

Answer: Contact support with the order number, item being returned, and reason for the return. Keep the item and its packaging until return eligibility is confirmed and return instructions or a shipping label are provided.

---

## Refund has not appeared

Resolution-ID: 91000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000005
Category: returns

Question: My return was accepted, but I have not received my refund.

Answer: Confirm that the returned item was received and that a refund was issued. Banks and card providers may take several business days to post an issued refund. If the expected posting period has passed, contact support with the order and return details.

---

## Duplicate charge

Resolution-ID: 91000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000006
Category: billing

Question: Why was I charged twice for the same order?

Answer: One entry may be a temporary authorization rather than a completed charge. Compare the amounts and transaction states in the payment account. If both entries have settled, contact support with the order number, charge dates, and amounts. Never send a full card number or security code.

---

## Payment was declined

Resolution-ID: 91000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000007
Category: billing

Question: Why is my payment being declined?

Answer: Verify the billing address, expiration date, available funds, and any restrictions imposed by the card provider. Try the payment only after checking those details. If it continues to fail, contact the payment provider or use another supported payment method. Support cannot override a bank decline.

---

## Cannot sign in

Resolution-ID: 91000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000008
Category: account

Question: I cannot sign in to my account.

Answer: Confirm the email address, request a password-reset link, and check spam or junk folders. Use only the newest reset link because older links may expire. If access still fails, contact support without sharing a password, one-time code, or recovery secret.

---

## Received a damaged product

Resolution-ID: 91000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000009
Category: product

Question: My item arrived damaged. What should I do?

Answer: Stop using the item if damage could make it unsafe. Keep the product and packaging, photograph the damage, and contact support with the order number and a description of the problem. Replacement or return options depend on the product and order details.

---

## Received the wrong item

Resolution-ID: 91000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000010
Category: product

Question: The item I received is not what I ordered.

Answer: Keep the item and packaging and compare the received product with the order confirmation. Contact support with the order number, the item received, and the item expected. Wait for return instructions before shipping anything back.
